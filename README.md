# HEIC Converter

Fast, clean, and ad-free HEIC to JPG converter for Android 9.0+.

Tired of HEIC files that are incompatible with your apps or devices? **HEIC Converter** offers a high-performance, streamlined solution to convert your photos into the universal JPG format.

## 🚀 Why choose HEIC Converter?

* **Zero Distractions:** 100% free of intrusive ads, ensuring you can work without interruptions.
* **High-Efficiency Batch Processing:** Process up to 20 images simultaneously with a single tap.
* **Smart Localization:** Automatically adapts its interface to your device language (English, French, Arabic, Turkish, etc.).
* **100% Offline & Private:** Your photos are processed locally on your device. No internet connection required, and your data remains completely private.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Requirements

* **Language:** Kotlin
* **UI Toolkit:** Jetpack Compose
* **Compatibility:** Requires Android 9.0 or higher.
* **Architecture:** Scoped Storage processing

## ⚙️ How to Build / Compile

To build this project from source, follow these steps:

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Husso73/HeicConverter.git
1. **Open in Android Studio:**
   * Open Android Studio and select *Open an Existing Project*.
   * Choose the cloned folder.
2. **Sync Gradle:**
   * Let Android Studio download dependencies and sync Gradle files automatically.
3. **Run the App:**
   * Connect an Android device (Android 9.0+) or start an Emulator.
   * Click the **Run 'app'** button (green play icon) in Android Studio.
